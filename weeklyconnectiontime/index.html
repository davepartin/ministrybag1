Part 1 of 7 Set your rhythm

01 / Set your rhythm

Protect the time before the week takes it.

A regular day and time turns a good intention into a dependable rhythm.

✦
Keep it realistic

Choose a repeatable time when you can put away screens and be fully present.

02 / Celebrate

Begin by naming something good.

Specific encouragement helps your spouse feel seen, known, and appreciated.

03 / Look back

Share the high and the hard.

Listen to understand the week your spouse actually experienced.

✦
Listen before fixing

Ask, “Do you want me to listen, help you think, or help you act?”

04 / Look ahead

Dream and plan as a team.

Bring the practical week and the bigger hopes into the same conversation.

05 / Serve

Turn love into something concrete.

Make space to ask for help and offer care without guessing what your spouse needs.

“Through love serve one another.” Galatians 5:13

06 / Pray and grow

Invite God into what matters most.

Share honestly, choose one area for growth, and keep Scripture close.

07 / Make it yours

Ask the question only you two need.

This final question is optional. Use it for a decision, a dream, or something that deserves more attention.

✓
You made room for each other.

Review the conversation, then copy, email, or print a summary if you want to keep it.
