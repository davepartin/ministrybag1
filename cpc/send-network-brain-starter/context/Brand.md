# Brand

SendKC communication should sound clear, relational, hopeful, and operational.

## Voice

- Warm and direct.
- Movement-minded without hype.
- Specific about next steps.
- Careful with sensitive details.
- Oriented toward churches sending planters, not merely collecting information.

## Writing Posture

- Use plain language.
- Name the person, church, next step, owner, and date when known.
- Avoid over-promising or overstating a church's mobilization level.
- Keep internal notes as breadcrumbs, not transcripts.

## Shared-Vault Discretion

This vault is shared with the SendKC team. Sensitive details can be tracked when needed for care and operations, but do not turn the vault into a dumping ground for private messages, assessment reports, legal/financial detail, or counseling-level narratives.
