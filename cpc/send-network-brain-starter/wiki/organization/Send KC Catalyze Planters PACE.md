# Catalyze Planters — PACE

The planter development framework is organized around **PACE**: Prepare, Assess, Care, and Equip. It keeps planter development from becoming only relational encouragement or only administrative process. A healthy pathway needs both.

## PACE Overview

| Letter | Area | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| P | Prepare | Initial development, discovery, qualification, and foundational setup. |
| A | Assess | Formal assessment and qualification process. |
| C | Care | Ongoing support and pastoral care for approved planters. |
| E | Equip | Training, coaching, and practical preparation for launch and growth. |

## Prepare — Discover and Discern

Initial contact, qualification, and foundational setup for potential planters.

- Meet with planters interested in planting, including a regional overview.
- Respond to cold calls from potential planters and leaders.
- Communicate planter expectations.
- Confirm Baptist Faith and Message 2000 compliance.
- Confirm NAMB Code of Conduct agreement.
- Prepare planters before the assessment retreat.
- Walk through the 9 Essentials evaluation process.
- Give SBC affiliation guidance for state and local associations.
- Review the Planter Benefits Package.

## Assess — Application and Evaluation

Formal assessment and evaluation for church planter candidates.

- Coordinate pre-assessment, including CPIA.
- Prepare for and follow up after the assessment retreat.
- Complete the Planter Profile Questionnaire (PPQ).
- Track assessment documentation.
- Discuss final reports with planter, spouse, and sending church.
- Include a two-day assessment retreat with spouse when applicable.

## Care — Ongoing Support

Ongoing pastoral care and support for approved planters.

- Set up Send Network Care for planters.
- Maintain regular relational check-ins, not just when problems surface.
- Monthly planter lunches.
- Annual planter-only appreciation event.
- Annual planter family event.
- Connect spouses with spouse care, retreats, and coaching.

## Equip — Training, Coaching, and Launch

Training and practical preparation for a healthy church plant.

- Send Network Orientation.
- Send Network Training.
- Annual multi-day planter retreat.
- Quarterly reports with assigned planters (typically 10–15 per CPC).
- Help planters build planting and support teams, budgets, and timelines.
- Discuss fundraising and clarify startup funds.
- Set up coaching or mentorship at the sending church.
- Review the Church Planter Master Checklist.

## Coaching Interpretation

- **Prepare:** help potential planters understand calling, place, doctrine, expectations, and next steps.
- **Assess:** help planters and spouses face reality with courage and clarity.
- **Care:** help planters know they are not alone.
- **Equip:** help planters build teams, budgets, habits, and coaching structures that survive launch.
