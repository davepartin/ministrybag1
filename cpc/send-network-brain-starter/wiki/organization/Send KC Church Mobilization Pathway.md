# Church Mobilization Pathway

A shared map for moving churches from basic cooperation to movement-level multiplication. It gives language for coaching churches, associations, and pastors toward deeper engagement in church planting. Set a church's `mobilization_level` from evidence, not optimism.

## Five Levels At A Glance

| Level | Name | Summary | The CPC's job |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cooperating | Basic cooperation and partnership; open to learning. | Invite to events. Start prayer for planters. Introduce planting through resources and stories. |
| 2 | Supporting | Regular prayer and financial support; building awareness. | Help establish sustainable support. Create prayer teams. Send members to Sending Labs. Begin spotting potential planters. |
| 3 | Sending | Identifies, trains, assesses, and sends its own planters. | Develop planter identification. Launch a residency. Establish coaching and care structures. |
| 4 | Multiplying | Has planted multiple churches with reproducible processes. | Help expand to new contexts. Mentor other churches. Build leadership pipelines. |
| 5 | Movement | Leads regional church planting movements. | Catalyze regional strategy. Create partnerships. Establish movement-level training. |

## Level 1: Cooperating
Willing to work with others in basic cooperative mission. Often early in understanding planting but open.
**Next steps:** attend Send Network events; begin systematic prayer for planters; learn through resources and conferences; connect with like-minded churches; invite a planter to share their story.

## Level 2: Supporting
Actively supports planting through prayer, finances, and encouragement while building congregational awareness.
**Next steps:** establish sustainable financial support; create a prayer team; attend Sending Labs; begin identifying potential planters in the congregation.

## Level 3: Sending
Identifies, trains, assesses, and sends its own members as planters, owning the multiplication process.
**Next steps:** build formal planter identification; launch a residency or internship; create financial and prayer support systems; establish ongoing coaching and care.

## Level 4: Multiplying
Has sent multiple planters and runs systematic, reproducible processes.
**Next steps:** expand to new regions and contexts; mentor other churches toward sending; develop leadership pipelines and training centers.

## Level 5: Movement
Leads and catalyzes broader movements, influencing regions and networks.
**Next steps:** develop movement-level training and resources; create strategic partnerships; pursue regional gospel saturation.

## Associations and Partners

Track your local associational relationships and their Directors of Mission / Associational Missional Strategists, plus seminary and denominational partners. Replace with your region's associations as you make this vault yours.

## Coaching Interpretation

The pathway keeps mobilization from becoming vague inspiration. It becomes a staircase: Cooperate → Support → Send → Multiply → Catalyze movement. A pastor may say "we are not a church planting church." The pathway lets you say, "You may be a cooperating church now, and that is a real starting point. What would one faithful next step look like?"
