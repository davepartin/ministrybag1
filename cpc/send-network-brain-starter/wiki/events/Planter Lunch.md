# Planter Lunch

> **Sample recurring event playbook.** A monthly gathering for planters. Adapt the day and place to your region.

## Mission & Core Purpose
- A regular, low-pressure table where planters are known, encouraged, and connected. Fellowship first; coordination second.

## General Details & Flow
- Monthly, a set weekday (many teams use the 3rd Thursday).
- A shared meal, a brief encouragement, prayer, and open connection.

## Standard Roles & Responsibilities
- Host and invite: the CPC team.
- Follow-up: note who came and who to check on.

## When (Months)
Ongoing (monthly)
