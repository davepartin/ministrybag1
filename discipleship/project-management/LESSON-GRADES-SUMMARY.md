## The template lessons (the bar we shoot for)

A template lesson gives a learner something to think about, talk about, learn and do. It has one big truth in a sentence, grace before command, one story and one tool that teach that same truth, 3 to 5 questions, a concrete practice, a Lesson Review, the standard closing trio, and about 1,000 to 1,500 words.
