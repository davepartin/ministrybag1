# Series editing guide: cadence and balance

Source: main 2940ab2, November 12, 2026. Read alongside the 37-lesson review chart. No lesson text or grade is changed by this guide.

## Use this while editing

Keep one story, one repeatable phrase, one reusable picture/exercise, Scripture explained in plain speech, personal response and a way to pass it on. Compare tone with 201-01 and Dave's approved 202-08, 202-09 and 203-06. Keep grace before pressure and paragraphs connected. Harder subjects can take more space. Do not flatten every lesson into the same length or sentence pattern.

The table counts prose/parable paragraphs before Closing Reflections, splitting HTML line breaks and excluding verse blocks, questions and widget text. A short-sentence cluster means at least three consecutive sentences of seven words or fewer. These are mechanical editing signals, not a reading-level test, proof of poor writing or a reason to rewrite every flagged paragraph. Abbreviations can affect sentence splitting. Long paragraphs may be intentional.

Claude owns 201-05, 201-10 and 202-01 tonight. Read those from his completed PRs before editing; this snapshot predates his work. Preserve the Learning Circle's depth and 203-08's accepted length. Do not change 101-06 under this packet.
