# One cross across the 200 series: plan

Date: November 13, 2026. Author: Claude, from Dave's spoken direction. Status: plan for Dave's review. No lesson text, widget or question has been changed by this document.

## Dave's direction

The Greatest Commandment is shaped like a cross, and the same drawn cross should open each of the three 200 courses, so the courses have synergy without repeating every lesson.

| Part of the cross | Course | Meaning | Word |
|---|---|---|---|
| Upright beam | 201 Growing Deep | God's love comes down first; your love goes back up to Him | Receive and respond |
| Left arm | 202 Growing Up | Love for the people closest to you: your home and your church family | Belong (Neighborhood Church language) |
| Right arm | 203 Growing Out | Love for people who do not yet know Jesus | Go |

Series line, ending every version of the widget: **"Love comes down, goes up, and reaches out."**

The cross is drawn like a marker sketch instead of filled shapes. The upright's left edge is drawn downward (God's love comes down) and its right edge upward (your love goes up), then each arm is drawn outward. 201-01 introduces the whole cross. 202-01 reviews the upright and focuses on the Belong arm. 203-01 reviews the upright and Belong, then draws the Go arm. Cross the Road leaves 202-01. Widget brief for Gemini: [2026-11-13-cross-widget-brief.md](2026-11-13-cross-widget-brief.md).

Decisions made in the plan, which Dave can change: Belong is the left arm and Go is the right. The Good Samaritan stays as 202-01's story: in version 2 the whole crossbeam lights first, because the Samaritan's love has no boundary, and then the focus moves to Belong. In 203-01 the cross is a short opener and the Personal Ministry Map stays the working tool, an intentional two-widget exception.
