# Whole-series review of Growing Together

This is a review, not an edit. Claude should take this to Dave, then change only what Dave approves. Question IDs must not be changed or reused. Retired questions must keep their saved answers. Approved wording is marked wherever a recommendation would reopen it.

Word counts below are story-through-review, learner-facing prose (headings, teaching, questions, boxes). Verse quotations are not counted. A 30-minute lunch conversation usually means about 15 to 20 minutes of reading, then room to talk. Most of these lessons already fit that window.

The live site was reached and walked on a phone-sized screen (390x844) across 19 lessons, including widgets. Hashes `#101-1` and `#101-01` both load.

## 1. The big picture

The series is closer than it feels from inside the work. The bones are right. 101 starts a person. 201 roots them in the Father, the Son, the Spirit, the Word, grace, and the way home when they drift. 202 turns that love toward neighbors, home, church, gifts, pain, praise, possessions, covenant, purity, and the table. 203 sends them out with a name, a map, and BLESS. The remember line is true: receive from God, give to others. The larger shape is true: love comes down, goes up, and reaches out. A six-month believer can walk this. A longtime churchgoer who has never been discipled one on one will finally get handles they have never had: Stop, Turn, Walk; GUIDE; OIA; the three R's; RUN; their one; SPACES.

What is still missing is not another course. It is one voice saying the same thing all the way through. "What you receive, you give away" is currently spoken out loud in only four lessons: 201-01, 202-07, 203-03, and 203-04. "Love comes down, goes up, and reaches out" bookends Growing Deep and the finale, then goes quiet in between. 101 never names the series theme at all. A learner can finish 37 lessons and still not be able to say the one sentence the series is built on.

The second gap is competition inside a few lessons, not emptiness. 201-06, 201-05, 202-03, 202-05, and 203-05 still ask the learner to hold more than one or two ideas. 101-05 and 202-03 both spend a long time on Acts 2 and "the church is a family." 201-10 has only two mid-lesson questions, under the three-to-five rule. A handful of voice-rule breaks remain, the clearest being "below" in 203-05, "Sit with the Scripture" in 101-01, and a trail of "Notice..." coaching lines Dave already asked the lesson not to use.

The greatest strengths are the stories, the grace order, and the widgets that a person could actually draw on a napkin. The running father, the robe, the roof, the widow's coins, Moses' shining face, the towel, Elisha's table: these are alive. The gospel is grace-first almost everywhere. Assurance is clear in 101-07. Forgiveness never asks someone to stay in danger. Dave's line-by-line work from mid-November on is visible. Do not reopen those lessons to make them "more consistent." Put the remember line in a few strategic places, cut what still competes, fix the remaining rule breaks, and leave the rest alone.

What would make the biggest difference in the month remaining: say the theme in 101, in the 201-08 mercy cycle, in the 202-01 neighbor turn, in the 203-01 send-off, and in the 203-10 ending, then streamline the five lessons that still feel like two lessons. That is a small amount of new wording and a medium amount of cutting. It is not a rewrite of the series.

## 2. Top 10 recommendations

Ranked by impact on the learner. Effort is for Claude after Dave says yes.

### 1. Speak the remember line at five turns in the journey

**What:** Add "What you receive, you give away" (and, where it already fits, "Love comes down, goes up, and reaches out") at the five turns a learner will actually remember: the start of sharing in 101-06, the mercy cycle in 201-08, the neighbor turn in 202-01, the send-off in 203-01, and the finale in 203-10. One sentence each. Do not add a new section. Do not retell the theme in every lesson.

**Why it matters:** Right now the series *is* one journey in the architecture and *reads* like 37 strong lessons. Learner A will not connect 101-06's "come and see" to 203-04's towel unless someone says the same sentence both times. Learner B already knows the parts. The sentence is what they will reuse with someone else.

**Lessons:** 101-06, 201-08, 202-01, 203-01, 203-10. (201-01, 202-07, 203-03, and 203-04 already have it. Leave those four as written.)

**Effort:** small.

**Touches approved wording:** yes, all five.

Suggested placements (exact current, then proposed):

101-06, after "You do not have to be an expert to share Jesus. You only have to say 'come and see' and tell what He did for you."
Proposed add, same paragraph: "What you receive, you give away."

201-08, the cycle paragraph that now ends "Those who have been given grace become people who give grace."
Proposed add: "What you receive, you give away."

202-01, after "You do not love your neighbor out of an empty tank, but give away what the Father has already poured into you."
Proposed add: "What you receive, you give away."

203-01, after "Carried people become carriers."
Proposed add: "What you receive, you give away."

203-10, the paragraph that now ends "Love comes down, goes up, and reaches out."
Proposed add, before that last sentence: "What you receive, you give away."

### 2. Streamline 201-06 so the four-movement story can breathe

**What:** Keep Emmaus, the four movements, the widget, and 6.4 ("Where do you see Creation, Fall, Redemption, and Restoration in your own story?"). Cut the four echo stories (Noah, Joseph, Israel, Paul) down to one sentence of permission ("Many smaller stories inside the Bible echo this shape"), or keep one echo, not four. Drop or shrink "A Resource to Go Deeper" (the Bible Project link). Change the practice so it does not rebuild the teach-it question Dave already retired.

**Why it matters:** This is the longest 201 lesson (1,411 words) and it asks the learner to hold the Emmaus story, four movements, four extra Bible stories, their own story, a video, and a two-to-three minute retell. Learner A gets lost in the middle. Learner B has heard CFRR before and will skip. 6.4 already does the work. The practice currently says: "Then tell one person, a family member, a coworker, or a friend over coffee, the storyline of the whole Bible in two or three minutes." That is the retired teach-it question (201-06-4) living on as homework.

**Lessons:** 201-06.

**Effort:** medium.

**Touches approved wording:** yes (VOICE-201-06).

Current practice:
"This week, walk one Bible story you already know through the four movements. Then tell one person, a family member, a coworker, or a friend over coffee, the storyline of the whole Bible in two or three minutes."

Proposed practice:
"This week, walk one Bible story you already know through the four movements. Write one sentence for each: Creation, Fall, Redemption, Restoration."

### 3. Let 202-03 be the one-another lesson, not a second church-is-a-family lesson

**What:** In 202-03, recap Acts 2 in two sentences and point back to 101-05. Let the one-another cards carry the lesson. Replace the Baptist Faith and Message paragraph with one plain sentence about a local church, with no denominational label. 101-05 stays the aspen grove and the potluck. Do not rewrite 101-05.

**Why it matters:** Learner A just learned in 101-05 that the church is a family, not a building, with the same Acts 2:42-47 block. Four lessons into Growing Up they get the same passage and the same "not a building" claim, plus a Southern Baptist statement they may not belong to. Learner B will feel they are back in membership class. The fresh gift in 202-03 is "one another" and "once not a people." Protect that.

**Lessons:** 202-03 (primary). 101-05 (do not rewrite; maybe one forward pointer in its review if Dave wants).

**Effort:** medium.

**Touches approved wording:** yes (VOICE-202-03). 101-05 is also protected.

Current 202-03 BFM paragraph:
"The Baptist Faith and Message, a short statement of what Southern Baptist churches believe, describes a church as a local family of baptized believers who have committed to follow Jesus together, live under His Word, practice baptism and the Lord's Supper, use their God-given gifts, and take the gospel to the ends of the earth. In other words, church is not vague. It is local, committed, biblical, gifted, and on mission."

Proposed:
"A local church is a family of baptized believers who have committed to follow Jesus together, live under His Word, share baptism and the Lord's Supper, use their gifts, and take the gospel to the people around them. Church is not vague. It is local, committed, biblical, gifted, and on mission."

**Lessons:** 203-05, 101-01, 201-03. (201-10 already marks Luke 15:17 as NIV. Keep that pattern.)

**Effort:** small.

**Touches approved wording:** yes.

## 3. Series-level findings

### The journey is real

Course bridges work. 101-07 offers "Go to 201 Growing Deep." 201-10 offers "Go to 202 Growing Up." 202-10 offers "Go to 203 Growing Out." 203-10 offers "Back to courses," with no next-lesson button, which is correct because 301 is Coming Soon. Recaps name the last lesson. The 201 Trinity arc (Father, Son, Spirit) is clean. The 201 Bible arc (framework, story, skill) is clean. The 202 love-others arc (neighbors, home, church) is clean. The 203 BLESS arc (Begin, Listen and Eat, Serve, Share) is clean. 101-06's first taste of BLESS, pointing to 203, is exactly right.

Layered tools, not copies:
- Repentance: 101-07 Stop, Turn, Walk → 201-10 Learning Circle → 202-09 RUN. This is one of the best designs in the series. Keep it.
- Bible: 101-04 three Discovery Questions → 201-07 OIA. Named as daily bread and weekly feast. Keep it.
- Gospel: 101-02 ABCs → 203-05 3 Circles plus three stories. Keep it, and make the widget as simple as the ABCs.
- Church: 101-05 belong and serve → 202-03 one another and membership. The idea is right. The execution still reteaches. See Top 10 #3.
- Baptism: 101-02 take the step → 202-10 doorway and table. Different jobs. Keep both. 202-10's note for people baptized as infants is pastoral and needed.
- Greatest Commandment cross: 201-01 receive, 202-01 neighbor, 203-10 welcome home. This is the series' visual spine. Keep all three versions.

### Repetition that still competes

- **Time, talents, and treasure.** 201-04 names them as one of five roots. 202-07 owns them (widget, heading, review). 203-04 used to own them and no longer does; the live lesson has one passing sentence ("Your time, your skills, your money, your voice"). The retired 203-04-3 still asks "Of your time, talents, and treasure..." That is correct ID preservation. Do not bring the three T's back into 203-04. The overlap Dave was worried about is already mostly solved.
- **Acts 2 and "church is a family."** 101-05 and 202-03. See Top 10 #3.
- **Emmaus.** 201-06 uses it for "the whole Bible is about Jesus." 203-03 uses it for listen and eat. Both are fair readings. Add one callback in 203-03 if Dave wants: "You walked this road in Growing Deep as the Bible's story. Today it is about a stranger invited in, and a table." Do not cut either story.
- **Matthew 7 builders.** 101-04 uses the wise builder for doing the Word. 202-02 uses it for a home on the rock. Fine. A callback is optional, not required.
- **Luke 15.** 201-01 father running, 201-02 robe, 201-10 pigpen and both sons. This is a feature, not a bug. 101-07 uses the lost sheep, a different Luke 15 story. Keep.

### Gaps

- The remember line is under-told. See Top 10 #1.
- 201-10 is short one question. See Top 10 #4.
- 203-10 tells the learner 301 is "on its way," which is right, but the last screen of the live lesson is the same "I have completed this lesson" box as every other lesson. The copy is already warm. The chrome does not celebrate. That is a product note, not a lesson rewrite.
- 203-02 has no interactive widget. The prayer card ("their life, their heart, my part") is napkin-simple as a box. Do not invent a widget unless Dave asks. The Daily Prayers tool link is the right next step.
- Menu subtitles and a few TOC rows are still builder language. See Top 10 #9.
