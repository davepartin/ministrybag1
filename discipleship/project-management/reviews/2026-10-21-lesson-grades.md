## How lessons were graded

The rubric comes from the Editorial and Visual gates in [PLAN.md](../PLAN.md#lesson-gates). Each lesson was scored A to F on eight criteria:

1. **Truth.** One big truth you could say in one sentence.
2. **Fit.** It teaches what the chapter title in [curriculum-toc.csv](../../curriculum-toc.csv) promises, and its scope is distinct from its neighbors.
3. **Scripture.** Accurate quotations and references, sound theology, and credible, attributed anecdotes.
4. **Grace.** Grace comes before command.
5. **Story and tool.** The story, image and widget teach the same truth, and a learner could explain the tool afterward.
6. **Step.** A concrete, realistic next step.
7. **Questions.** A reasonable question load, a Lesson Review, and the standard closing trio.
8. **Length.** About 1,000 to 1,500 words, roughly 25 minutes on a phone.

Calibration came from the five lessons Dave has already approved: 202-08, 202-09, 202-10, 203-06 and 203-09. Those define the "A" standard for voice, length and structure.

Each lesson also got a work level:

- **Ready:** read it and approve it.
- **Polish:** under an hour of edits.
- **Revise:** rewrite a section.
- **Rework:** rethink the lesson.

## Patterns across the courses

**Two eras of writing.** The newer lessons share one shape: a story block, one widget, 3 to 5 questions, a Lesson Review, and the standard closing trio. The older lessons (202-05, 06, 07 and 203-01, 02, 03, 04, 07) still have the old closing with the Daily Devotions question, and most have no Lesson Review. 202-05 and 202-06 also show the wrong lesson numbers on their questions (9.x and 5.x) and open by recapping 201. All of this is mechanical cleanup that doesn't need Dave's voice.

**Missing stories and tools.** The TOC promises a story that never appears in 201-05 (Josiah), 201-06 (Emmaus), 201-09 (the Vine), 202-03 (Acts 2), and 202-05 and 202-06, where Joseph and Paul and Silas appear only as text. 203-01, 02 and 03 have no story at all. Seven lessons have no working tool: 202-03, 202-05, 202-06, 203-01, 203-02, 203-03 and 203-04. Two of those, 203-01 and 203-03, show a "[PLACEHOLDER]" line to learners. 202-01 reuses 201-01's widget.

**Translation.** The app footer and 201-05 both say the course uses the ESV, but several quotations are not ESV:

- **NIV:** 201-03 (Exodus 13:21), 201-10 (Luke 15:17), 202-07 (Psalm 24:1), 203-07 (Luke 10:19), and the grace-diagram and learning-circle widgets.
- **NLT:** 202-02 (1 Timothy 3:4-5).
- **Mixed or other wording:** 203-03 (Matthew 5:16 and 1 Peter 4:10).
- **"Lord" where the ESV prints "LORD":** 201-07 (Psalm 23) and 201-10 (Isaiah 53:6).

**Too long.** 201-04 (2,523 words), 201-09 (2,318), 202-01 (2,208), 203-05 (about 1,990), 203-04 (about 1,960) and 203-03 (about 1,860).

**Repetition across lessons.**

- John 15 appears in both 201-04 and 201-09.
- Genesis 50:20 appears in 201-06, 201-09 and 202-05.
- Lament and Psalm 42 appear in 202-05 and 202-06.
- 202-02's spouse section duplicates 202-08.
- 2 Timothy 2:2 and multiplication appear in 203-01 and 203-08.
- 203-02 pre-teaches 203-03, 04 and 05.
- Learners meet four different circle diagrams: 202-01, 203-01, 203-06 and 203-09.

**Theology and accuracy calls that only Dave should make.**

- **201-09:** the vine and vinedresser roles are reversed.
- **201-04:** "Jesus is the Good Soil."
- **202-05:** "God did not cause it, He directed it" on Genesis 50:20.
- **202-04:** APEST presented as Paul's plain meaning.
- **201-08:** the definition of justification.
- **201-03:** "the whole Trinity is visible."
- **201-06:** "you were made... then sin entered your life."
- **202-07:** a generosity case without the cross.
- **202-01:** the Luke 10 sequence.
- **203-10:** Bezalel as "the first person God named."

## Suggested order for Dave's personal work

These need Dave's voice, story choices or theology. They're in the order that buys the most improvement per hour.

1. **202-05, 202-06, 202-07.** Bring the three old-template 202 lessons up to the 202-08 standard. Each needs a carrying story (Joseph, Paul and Silas at midnight, the widow), one tool, and grace before command. 202-05 also needs the Genesis 50:20 fix.
2. **201-09 and 201-04.** Correct the vine and the soil, cut each to about 1,400 words, and give John 15 to 201-09 alone.
3. **202-01 and 202-03.** 202-01 needs the Luke 10 fix, a cut to length and its own tool. 202-03 needs Acts 2 as its story and a one-another tool.
4. **203-01.** Build one ministry map that 203-09 can build on, and remove the placeholder.
5. **201-05, 201-06, 201-07.** Add the Josiah and Emmaus stories, and make OIA the 201-07 tool.
6. **The Polish group.** Read and approve, applying the listed fixes as you go.

The cleanup that doesn't need Dave's voice can be handed to an agent: closing trios, Lesson Reviews, question numbering, ESV wording, placeholder lines, and the clear factual slips (Luke 10, Bezalel, Ephesus and Rome). Then Dave's reading time goes to substance.
