# 101 course review: tone, direction, stories, widgets and progression

Date: November 16, 2026. Reviewer: Claude, at Dave's request ("do not change the lessons"). Dave's response: "i love almost all of these ideas, so store them somewhere so you don't forget!" Work proceeds one lesson at a time, one part at a time, starting with 101-03. Nothing here is approved wording; each change still goes to Dave.

Standing rule from Dave (DAVE.md, November 16): 101 lessons do not need teach-it or "tell a friend" questions.

## Overall

The course reads like a pastor walking beside a new believer. The progression works: your story (01), how new life began (02), talking to God (03), listening to God (04), the family you belong to (05), inviting others in (06), what to do when you fall (07). Hand-offs between lessons are clean. Two threads tie it together: the garden picture (good soil, greenhouse, aspen grove, apple tree) and the John reading in every lesson. The lessons Dave reworked personally (02, 04, 07) are the strongest: short, one clear idea, grace-first. Most help needed: 03, then 05, then small items in 06.

## Lesson by lesson

**101-01 Welcome. Let's Start Growing.** Solid. Working: warm welcome, "a seed cannot make itself grow", Philippians 1:6 up front, the mentor section, Peaks and Valleys, and the note that some valleys still hurt. Consider: the paper-timeline box and the Peaks and Valleys widget explain the same exercise (less of a problem than 101-04 because the box is a paper task); the lesson opens with three questions in a row before any teaching.

**101-02 Salvation and Baptism.** Gold; leave alone. One loose end already in the 101 tracker: the text says "three letters, the ABCs... Each stop is one verse from Romans" while the widget now has D for Discipleship (Luke 9:23). A one-sentence bridge would fix it, only if Dave reopens the lesson.

**101-03 Prayer.** Needs the most help.
1. Story: two sentences and no art; the only 101 lesson after the first without a picture.
2. Voice: the six steps read like teaching notes, with all-caps titles ("ENGAGE IN SPIRITUAL WARFARE") and "we/us" wording ("God has adopted us", "When we proclaim who He is...") while the rest of the course speaks to "you". "Spiritual warfare" can sound intimidating in week three.
3. Tool: the practice sends people out of the app to the Daily Prayers site; every other lesson keeps its tool inside.
Direction: keep the six steps; rewrite them warmly in "you" language with plainer step names; add story art of Jesus praying early in the morning (Mark 1:35); an in-lesson six-step widget could come later.

**101-04 The Bible.** Strong after the November 16 simplification: Nicodemus with art, the widget, one real try on John 3. Nit: the review still says "share them with a friend", which Dave's rule makes optional.

**101-05 The Church.** Second most in need. Working: "You Already Belong" ("you do not earn your seat at the table, you were given one") and the service checklist. Issues: longest 101 lesson (1,184 words); two pictures compete (aspen grove and potluck); the opening recap is four sentences and restates the last lesson; 5.3 is a teach-it question ("If a friend said church is just a building, how would you explain the potluck to them?"). Direction: aspen grove as the story, potluck as the action, shorter recap, retire 5.3.

**101-06 Sharing Jesus with Others.** Good heart, rough edges; Dave's earlier edits protected. Issues: "Tap through below for a first taste" breaks the series rule against "tap" and "below"; 6.2 is awkward and asks two things ("List three people who can be a part of your personal ministry inviting to 'Come and See'... and also you can start to BLESS"); its definition of personal ministry ("sharing the good news with people you know and people you meet") differs from 203-01's ("loving the people God has placed in your life and helping them know and follow Jesus") and the two should match. Working: the woman at the well, "Jesus came to her first", "Come and see is still the whole invitation."

**101-07 When You Drift.** Strong after the rewrite and Dave's edits: the shepherd art, "You do not obey to stay saved. You obey because you are saved.", the Stop, Turn, Walk widget, Peter in John 21. Nit: the review paragraph is a little long.

## Suggested order

101-03 (voice, story, tool), then 101-05 (one picture, shorter, retire 5.3), then a quick 101-06 cleanup (the tap line, 6.2, the personal ministry definition).
