## Read the statuses

- **Recorded:** explicit approval applies and the current component matches the approved version. Daniel includes the separately approved age-only correction.
- **Partial:** specific edits, frames, or selection were approved; the whole current component still needs final review.
- **Recheck:** an earlier approval record exists, but the current component differs.
- **Unrecorded:** no separate final sign-off was found. This does not prove Dave never reviewed it.
- **N/A:** no embedded widget or no lesson image is present. It is not approval and may represent an intended exercise or a remaining visual gap.
- **Accepted longer:** Dave explicitly accepted the longer 203-08 lesson; no exact numeric count was approved.

## Counting method

Lesson words run from recap through review/transition, stopping before Closing Reflections. Counts include headings, mid-lesson question prompts, Scripture text and references, and box text. They exclude the title/summary, closing reflections, assigned John reading blocks, embedded-widget text, image alt and filenames. HTML is removed and whitespace-delimited words are counted. Counts therefore differ from earlier reports that used another boundary. The 101 lessons additionally assign three John chapters each, outside these counts. Roughly 1,400 words is a guide, not automatic approval.
