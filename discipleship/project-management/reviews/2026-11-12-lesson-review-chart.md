## Read the statuses

- **Recorded:** explicit approval applies and the current component matches the approved version. Daniel includes the separately approved age-only correction.
- **Partial:** specific edits, frames, or selection were approved; the whole current component still needs final review.
- **Recheck:** an earlier approval record exists, but the current component differs.
- **Unrecorded:** no separate final sign-off was found. This does not prove Dave never reviewed it.
- **N/A:** no embedded widget or no lesson image is present. It is not approval and may represent an intended exercise or a remaining visual gap.
- **Accepted longer:** Dave explicitly accepted the longer 203-08 lesson; no exact numeric count was approved.

## Counting method

Lesson words run from recap through review/transition, stopping before Closing Reflections. Counts include headings, mid-lesson question prompts, Scripture text and references, and box text. They exclude the title/summary, closing reflections, assigned John reading blocks, embedded-widget text, image alt and filenames. HTML is removed and whitespace-delimited words are counted. Counts therefore differ from earlier reports that used another boundary. The 101 lessons additionally assign three John chapters each, outside these counts. Roughly 1,400 words is a guide, not automatic approval.

## Grades and their meaning

Codex refreshed the existing ten-checkpoint [production rubric](2026-11-12-aaa-37-scorecard.md) against this snapshot. Each checkpoint earns 0, 5 or 10. The grade describes the lesson package: teaching/focus, early story, memorable phrase, reusable tool, discussion questions, teach-it/transfer, ending, readability, artwork delivery and shared technical evidence. It is separate from Dave's wording, theological, art and release approvals. An A+ package still needs those gates.

Scale: **100 = AAA (A+), 95 = A, 90 = A-, 85 = B+, 80 = B, 75 = B-, 70 = C+, 65 = C, 60 or lower = C-.** These are the project's production bands, not school percentages. No current lesson falls below B-.

Unchanged checkpoints retain the earlier audit judgment; changed openings, questions, counts, artwork and integrated widgets were checked. This is not a new exhaustive theological or physical-device/pilot audit. The Grade detail sheet and evidence JSON show all ten checkpoint scores and the earlier score.

Five prompts in 101-04 are intentionally treated as one guided three-question practice plus a reading reflection and teach-it (Q=10). This is a reviewer judgment, not new Dave approval. Keep 201-10's depth and challenge: its lower grade concerns flow, repeated setup, transfer and pending widget review. 203-08 keeps Dave's accepted-longer exception without a length deduction. Valley Road still counts as a fitting 202-05 exercise; no new mosaic widget is required. The static original 203-07 sketch receives partial art-format points without rejecting Dave's artwork.
