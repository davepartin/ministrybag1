## How Percentages and Grades Work

Ten checkpoints, each worth ten points. Give ten for present and meeting the production checkpoint, five for partial/queued polish, and zero for missing. Add the points to get the percentage. This is a deliberately coarse editorial estimate, not a measured percentage of engineering time, learner comprehension, or release approval.

| Code | Checkpoint | What Was Checked |
|---|---|---|
| T | Teaching and focus | Substantial, focused teaching with grace and a practical direction, read against existing review evidence. Not a new verse-by-verse theological audit. |
| S | Opening story | A clear early narrative; the welcome lesson can use its course-opener exception. A later Bible practice passage earns partial credit, not an early-story pass. |
| P | Memorable phrase | One repeatable idea, not several competing handles. |
| V | Visual/reusable tool | A fitting widget, checklist or exercise. A specifically queued older-widget review is partial, not a claim the widget is broken. No widget is invented just to fill a box. |
| Q | Discussion questions | Three or four short, standalone questions. About twenty words is guidance, not an exact twenty-word cut-off; related diagnosis/action pairs can stay one clear question. Five questions or clearly overlong wording is partial. |
| X | Teach-it / pass-it-on | Explain the idea in one's own words or name a concrete recipient/action that carries it to someone. A private practice, prayer-name list or observer alone is weaker than explicit transfer. |
| R | Review and ending | A coherent review, key/step/prayer closing questions, plus Daily Devotions in 101. |
| L | Readability and length | Main lesson near 1,400 words or less. A longer draft that needs a trim is partial; Dave's accepted longer 203-08 is an exception, not a fresh trim assignment. |
| A | Story artwork | Ten for sequence/dissolve art; five for a plain picture needing an upgrade; zero for no story art. This checks production presence/delivery, not Dave's personal art approval. |
| C | Shared technical checks | Required foundation/export/storage/asset checks and current all-course browser delivery/layout checks. This is not proof of every widget state, physical Safari, sign-in or cross-device behavior. |

Project grading scale: **100 = AAA (A+)**, 95 = A, 90 = A-, 85 = B+, 80 = B, 75 = B-, 70 = C+, 65 = C, 60 or lower = C-. These are project finish grades, not school marks or ratings of biblical worth. Remaining polish and final pastoral/visual/pilot gates still need their own evidence, even at 100.

Question wording in the table is a proposal queue only. Never replace a Dave-approved question or rename a saved-answer ID to increase a score.
