# SWEEP-003: shorten long and two-part mid-lesson questions

Date: 2026-11-11. Owner: Claude (Fable 5.1). Branch: `claude/sweep-questions`.

## Why

Dave's rule: one question, one line, aimed at the heart of the lesson. The series review found 15 lessons with at least one mid-lesson question over 25 words, several asking two or three things at once, and a few that restate the lesson before asking. This sweep rewrites 32 questions in 17 lessons. Numbering, IDs, heights and positions are unchanged; only the question text changed.

Left alone on purpose: 202-09 (Dave marked 9.1 to 9.4 final as written), 203-08 (Dave's own questions; they belong to the 203-08 trim, done one lesson at a time), closing questions (key, step, prayer, devos), and every question already at or under about 20 words that asks one thing.
