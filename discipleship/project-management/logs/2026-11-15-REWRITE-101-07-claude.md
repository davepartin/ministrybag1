## Assignment

Dave, November 15: 101-07 is too long; it is the 101 taste of what is coming, and 201-10 already teaches the Learning Circle. It should give general starting information about repentance and dovetail with seeking holiness and fighting sin (202-09 covers purity). He likes the "Jesus comes to rescue" start and is open to rethinking the rest. The theology he wants: you do not stay saved by being perfect; good works do not save you or keep you saved, that is all Jesus; but Jesus says if you love me you will obey me, so a heart to obey displays that you are saved. Repentance is the Christian life: you fall down, and you can get back up because Jesus is there like a loving Father. Many readers will talk about sin with a mentor for the first time, so the lesson must leave room for questions and answers.
