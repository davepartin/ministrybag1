## Assignment

Dave, overnight November 14: 203-01 "needs a rewrite"; make it a AAA opener for the course on loving the lost and having a personal ministry, helping people "learn to think about others" before the practical BLESS lessons. He supplied notes from his own sermons: "God is growing our faith so He can work through us for the faith of others"; the definition "Your personal ministry is loving the people God has placed in your life and helping them know and follow Jesus"; the phrases "Love has names", "Think beyond your tree. Pray for the orchard", "Share your life. Share the good news"; and the question "Does everyone who knows me know the good news of Jesus?" He said these were ideas "to help you decide".

## Decisions

- Phrase: "Love has names." It leads straight into the Personal Ministry Map, where the learner writes names.
- Story: Four Friends and a Roof (Mark 2:1-12), used nowhere else in the series. "When Jesus saw their faith" carries Dave's thesis of faith for the faith of others, and the friends carrying one man gives the lesson its grace line: "Your part is to carry. His part is to heal." Dave's notes suggested the woman at the well; she is already the story of 101-06 (Come and See) and appears in 202-06, so she was not repeated here.
- The mountain and Matthew 28 stay as a teaching section (the lesson title and key verse are the Great Commission), with "some doubted" kept from the old draft.
- The orchard: one section, "Think Beyond Your Tree", built on the series tree picture (a tree does not eat its own fruit). 203-08 already ends with an orchard, so this plants what that lesson harvests. 1 Corinthians 3:6 is left to 203-05 and 203-08.
- Grace before the call: identity first, "not a sales pitch or a quota", "the overflow of a heart Jesus has rescued".
- Kept from the old lesson: course welcome, logo, the Studio Wash cross opener (great-command-v5-203), the Matthew 28:18-20 and Acts 1:8 verse blocks, the Personal Ministry Map widget and its position, the names question, the BLESS preview, the next-lesson line and the closing trio.
- Left out: the three anchors as a list, the John 20:21 verse block (it is on the cross opener's card and in 203-08), the "Christians should be the missionaries" paragraph (its point survives as "Jesus does not tell His followers to wait and hope people come to them"), the Deuteronomy 6:7 home paragraph (on the map card), the mountain-bike and concert-ticket illustrations and the neighbor's-baby story (personal stories stay out of lessons), and quotations already used later (Piper in 203-04, the Listen line in 203-03, the prayer line in 203-02).

Lesson words 1,299 before, 1,283 after (review-chart method). Four mid-lesson questions.
