# SWEEP-002: outside-author quotes to credit lines, and the 101 closing trio

Date: 2026-11-11. Owner: Claude (Fable 5.1). Branch: `claude/sweep-author-credits`.

## Why

Dave's rule from the 202-08 and 202-09 revisions: retell outside authors in the lesson's own voice and credit them in a plain line at the bottom, never quote them by name in the body. Five lessons still named an author mid-lesson. Separately, the seven 101 lessons closed with "What is one key thing you learned from this lesson, and what next step can you take this week?" in one question, while every 200-series lesson asks those as two questions. This sweep aligns both.

### 101 closing trio

In 101-01 through 101-07, `N-key` now reads "What is one key thing you learned from this lesson?" and a new `N-step` question follows it: "What is one step you are going to take this week?" (same height as the key question). `N-devos` and `N-prayer` are unchanged, so 101 lessons close with four questions; the Daily Devotions question stays because 101 carries the John reading plan.
