| Section | What it does | Dave's material used |

## The mosaic and the widget (decision for Dave)

The mosaic is taught in prose only. The Valley Road widget is untouched and now sits under "The Valley Refines You," where it fits Dave's mountaintop and valley line. That leaves the lesson with a phrase (Trust the Artist) that its widget does not build.

Candidate brief for Gemini, only if Dave assigns it: one widget, widgets/mosaic.html. Opens zoomed on one dark, cracked, sharp piece of glass with the line "This is the piece you are holding." Three or four steps pull back: neighboring pieces, then a section, then the whole image. Final image candidates from Dave: a sunrise, an empty tomb, or a cross with the dark piece inside it. Last card: "You do not have to see the whole mosaic to trust the Artist." It must not promise that a learner will see the reason for a particular loss in this life. Follow widgets/widget.md and reduced-motion rules. Dave then chooses whether it replaces Valley Road or whether Valley Road stays and the mosaic sits after the Artist paragraph. curriculum-toc.csv still names the Valley Road and James 1:2-4 and was not changed.
