### Logo v2 (same PR, after Dave's review)

Main logo only, plus the sprout-leaf fix on the word-free app icon. Same branch, never force-pushed.

- Middle circle is three even thirds of the diameter (68px each on a 204px circle). Darkest green on top (Growing Out), medium in the middle (Growing Up), lightest at the bottom (Growing Deep), matching Dave's original. Bands clip to the circle and stay translucent so blue and coral overlaps blend softly (no multiply).
- The bands are the tree. A large rounded scalloped crown fills the top band and dips just into the middle. A single strong trunk runs the middle third. Roots spread wide just below the lower boundary. Crown, trunk, and roots are separate closed paths (stroke only for now, so a fill can be added later without a silhouette change yet).
- White line work and fruit on the dark top band; charcoal everywhere else. White 'Growing Out'; charcoal for the other labels.
- Words are Inter Medium converted to paths: Starting / Leading near the top of the side circles (single words; the two-line 'in Discipleship' form did not keep good margins). Growing Out / Growing Up / Growing Deep sit in clear space and do not collide with lines.
- Sprout: two pointed almonds with center veins, a stem, and spreading roots, sized like the full thirds reference. Orchard: three scalloped trees, center taller, each with a trunk, roots, and fruit. Thin-line style kept; no filled silhouettes (Dave has not chosen that yet).
- App icon and favicons stay word-free. The app-icon sprout uses the same pointed veined leaves. Venn fills are translucent, not multiply.
- og-card (1200x630) regenerated with the v2 logo at 980px plus 'Growing Together Discipleship Series'. Band words stay legible at link-preview size.
- On-page logo is still `images/brand/gt-logo.svg` at 540px / 92%. Checked at 375, 390, and 1280. Words remain readable at 390px (logo display about 359px), so there is no text-free mobile variant.

`scripts/build_lesson_images.js` was not run: no jpg/png was added under `images/` (only `images/brand/`), so the generated lesson map would not change.
