### Logo v2 (same PR, after Dave's review)

Main logo only, plus the sprout-leaf fix on the word-free app icon. Same branch, never force-pushed.

- Middle circle is three even thirds of the diameter (68px each on a 204px circle). Darkest green on top (Growing Out), medium in the middle (Growing Up), lightest at the bottom (Growing Deep), matching Dave's original. Bands clip to the circle and stay translucent so blue and coral overlaps blend softly (no multiply).
- The bands are the tree. An 8-lobe even scalloped crown is about 70% of the previous width, centered in the top band, with its bottom just dipping into the middle. Every lobe stays in the green-only area. One charcoal stroke for the whole tree: a single trunk that forks into 3 limbs right at the crown base, each with one small sub-fork, all ending inside the crown, plus 6 fruit circles in the canopy. Roots are 6 curved strokes with small forks, kept inside about 60% of the circle width.
- Growing Out sits above the crown with clear space. Growing Up sits left of the trunk, centered in the middle band, with cap-height clearance from the trunk and the crown. All three Growing labels share one size. Growing Deep sits below the roots. White Growing Out; charcoal for the other labels.
- Sprout: two almond leaves angled out about 45 degrees from a slightly curved stem, with a gentle curve and tips pointing up and out. Roots on the sprout are unchanged. Same leaves on the app icon, scaled to about 40% of the icon height and centered on the triple-overlap. Orchard: three trees, center crown about 20% larger, each trunk has one small fork.
- og-card (1200x630) regenerated with the v2 logo at 980px plus 'Growing Together Discipleship Series'. Band words stay legible at link-preview size.
- On-page logo is still `images/brand/gt-logo.svg` at 540px / 92%. Checked at 375, 390, and 1280. Words remain readable at 390px (logo display about 359px), so there is no text-free mobile variant.

`scripts/build_lesson_images.js` was not run: no jpg/png was added under `images/` (only `images/brand/`), so the generated lesson map would not change.
