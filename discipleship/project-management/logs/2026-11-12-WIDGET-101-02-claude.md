# 101-02 Romans Road widget, rebuilt (WIDGET-101-02)

Date: 2026-11-12. Agent: Claude. Branch: claude/widget-romans-road2. Dave: "work on the widget for 101-02, it is an older one and it can be much better."

## What was weak

The old widgets/romans-road.html drew a pale landscape with an intro card floating over it, three label chips, and a card that was mostly a verse dump. Nothing in it carried the lesson's point (who does the work on the road) and nothing connected to the lesson's greenhouse picture.

## What the new one does

widgets/romans-road2.html, built on the A-standard layout from guide-spirit2 (title bar, scene, card, Back/dots/Next, gtFitCards, width-only refit).

- A road climbs from a dark valley at the bottom left to a lit gate under the sun at the top right. It fills in as you walk. Three signposts, A Admit, B Believe, C Confess, light up in turn, then a fourth stop, Saved, at the gate.
- Open hands sit at the start of the road and return at the end. At Saved, the lesson's green shoot in a clay pot appears, the same picture as the greenhouse parable above the widget.
- Each card: the stop's title, the verse with key words in bold, one "Your part / God's part" line, and the reference. The Saved card carries the lesson's own sentence: "They are empty hands receiving a gift Jesus already paid for."
- Captions: "Three stops. One gift." at the start; "You did not earn the road. You walked it with empty hands." at the end.
- Verses: Romans 3:23 and 6:23; 5:8; 10:9; 10:13 and 8:1. Same verses as before plus 10:13 and 8:1 for the Saved stop.
- Reduced motion respected. No timers, no DOM changes while idle, refit only on width change, per widget.md.

The old file is left in place; 101-02.json now points at romans-road2.html at the same 560px height.
