# SWEEP-001: remove tap, click and widget-naming language from lesson prose

Date: 2026-11-11. Owner: Claude (Fable 5.1). Branch: `claude/sweep-widget-language`.

## Why

Dave's series review (2026-11-11) found that about a third of the lessons still tell the reader to "tap Begin", "click through each step", or point at "the tool below" and "the diagram below". The rewritten lessons (202-08, 202-09, 202-10, 203-06, 203-09) state the idea in one line and let the widget follow. This sweep brings the rest of the series to that pattern. Dave's rule from the revision files: never name a widget in prose, never say tap or click.
