# 202-06 worship widget, rebuilt as the mirror angles (WIDGET-202-06)

Date: 2026-11-12. Agent: Claude. Branch: claude/widget-mirror-angles. Dave's direction in chat: a black-and-white progression of angles and mirrors; four clickable angles under the picture with dotted lines showing four ways to live; no mirror at all (we want to be seen), tilted toward created things, something in between, and rightly held so people see God; image bearers reflecting His qualities; ideas from Desiring God and his own.

## What the widget does

widgets/mirror-angles.html on the A-standard layout. Black ink on white. A sun (God's worth) top right, three onlookers bottom left, and a person in the center holding a mirror. Four angle buttons sit under the picture: 0° Me, 15° Things, 30° Sunday, 45° God. Dotted rays run from the sun to the mirror and onward; dotted sightlines show where the onlookers' eyes land.

- 0°, no mirror: arms up, the onlookers' sightline ends on the person. Romans 1:25. "A life with no mirror wants to be seen and keeps the glory."
- 15°, tilted to created things: a screen, a dollar sign and a trophy appear; the reflection and the sightline go there. 1 John 2:15.
- 30°, Sunday only: the mirror catches the sun for a moment and the ray curls back toward the person. Matthew 15:8. "Worship becomes an event, not a posture."
- 45°, held rightly: the ray from the sun bounces off the mirror to the onlookers, their sightline runs through the person up to the sun, and the sun glows. Matthew 5:16 and Genesis 1:27, with the lesson's own definition in bold: "Worship is gladly reflecting back to God the radiance of His worth."

Captions: "God is the sun. You are the mirror." at the start; "When they see you, they see Him." at the end. Labels under the figures: "the people around you", "you, an image bearer", "God's worth".

The old worship-is.html stays in the repo; 202-06.json points at the new widget at 640px.
