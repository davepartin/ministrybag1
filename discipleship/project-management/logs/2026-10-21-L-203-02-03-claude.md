## Why

The October 12 placement review (PR #50, `reviews/2026-10-12-203-placement.md`) found that 203-02 taught all five BLESS rhythms instead of Begin in Prayer, and that 203-03 was mostly hospitality with no real teaching on listening. Both lessons now teach what their chapter titles promise. Models were 203-06, 203-08 (PR #49) and 203-09.

## Changes and decisions

**203-02 BLESS - Begin in Prayer** (about 1,380 words, from 1,238):

- The journey recap names the BLESS arc (Lessons 2 to 5) with the BLESS logo. That is the short recap of the full rhythm. The five per-rhythm sections are gone.
- Story "A Prayer Before the Answer": Nehemiah prays for months, then prays silently before he answers the king (Nehemiah 1:4, 1:11, 2:2, 2:4, 2:8). The bridge lands on Dave's line "Talk to God about people before you talk to people about God."
- Why Prayer Comes First: John 5:17, 1 Timothy 2:1, 3-4, and Dave's breathe-in / breathe-out framing from the research note, with his two questions ("Where are You already working...? How can I join You?"). Luke 5:16 replaces the research note's John 12:29 citation, which looks like a typo for John 12:49.
- Tool: "Their Life, Their Heart, My Part" prayer card (a `box` block). Practices: tie prayer to existing habits, prayer walks, arrow prayers, "How can I pray for you?" and remembering to follow up. Colossians 4:2-3 corrected to current ESV.
- Grace close, "Prayer Is Not a Performance": Hebrews 7:25. Then a Lesson 2 Review, a next-lesson line, the standard closing trio, and a credit to Dave and Jon Ferguson's *BLESS*.
- Questions: 2.1 `203-02-story` (new), 2.2 `203-02-1` (kept, lightly edited), 2.3 `203-02-rhythm` (new), 2.4 `203-02-7` (kept, narrowed). Closing `203-02-key` (kept, now standard text), `203-02-step` (new), `203-02-prayer` (kept). Retired: `203-02-2` to `203-02-6` and `203-02-devos`.

**203-03 BLESS - Listen and Eat - Show Hospitality** (about 1,410 words, from 1,842):

- Story "Stay with Us": the Emmaus road (Luke 24:15-19, 29-32). Jesus asks questions and listens before He teaches, and He is recognized at the table. Key line: "Listening opened their hearts, and the table opened their eyes."
- Listen: James 1:19, Bartimaeus (Mark 10:51), and listening to the Spirit while listening to a person. Tool: "Ask, Ask Again, Remember" box, with Proverbs 20:5 (moved here from 203-02).
- Eat: Luke 7:34, Luke 15:1-2 (moved here from 203-02), *philoxenia*, Hebrews 13:2, Romans 12:13, entertaining vs hospitality, and "The Open Table" IS vs ISN'T box (the TOC's named tool). "Pull Up a Chair" gives practical invitations, receiving hospitality (Luke 10:7), and courage with Matthew 28:20.
- Grace close: Ephesians 2:12-13, 1 Peter 4:9-10 (key verse), Revelation 19:9. Then a Lesson 3 Review, a next-lesson line pointing to 203-04's foot washing, the closing trio, and credits (Fergusons; David Mathis and Matt Chandler, whose articles in the research note shaped the hospitality material).
- Removed: the unattributed "Diane" anecdote (it is Stacy Reaoch's story in her TGC article), the uncredited Steve Childers anecdote, the near-verbatim article phrasing, the `list` block (bullet-style prose) and the infographic placeholder.
- Corrected ESV quotations: Matthew 5:16 had NIV wording (now removed), and 1 Peter 4:10 had older "manifold grace of God" wording (now "God's varied grace").
- Questions: 3.1 `203-03-heard` (new), 3.2 `203-03-listen` (new), 3.3 `203-03-2` (kept, narrowed to the pressure to impress), 3.4 `203-03-6` (kept, tightened). Closing `203-03-key` (kept), `203-03-step` (new), `203-03-prayer` (kept). Retired: `203-03-1`, `-3`, `-4`, `-5` and `-devos`.
