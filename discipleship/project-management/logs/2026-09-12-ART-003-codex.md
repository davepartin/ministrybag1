### abramPrompt

Use case: illustration-story. Create one finished 16:9 wide black-and-white charcoal illustration for an adult Christian discipleship lesson, Genesis 15. Abram, an old bearded man in ancient modest robes, lies asleep on the ground at the edge of a desert camp at night. Beside him two facing rows of dark indistinct sacrificial shapes form a narrow path. A smoking earthen fire pot and a separate flaming torch pass alone along that path. Vast starry sky. Reverent, atmospheric and non-graphic: sacrifice pieces only suggested in distant dark silhouettes, no blood, no visible wounds. God is represented only by the smoking pot and torch, no human divine figure or angel; Abram must be asleep, not walking. Rough expressive charcoal, loose energetic pencil strokes, heavy cross-hatching, deep shadows and bright fire against textured paper, cinematic storyboard composition readable on a phone. Grayscale only, no lettering, borders, watermark or panels. Output one image, landscape 16:9.
