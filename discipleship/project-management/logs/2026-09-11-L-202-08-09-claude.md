## Changes and decisions

This packet responds to reviews/2026-09-11-202-08-09-drafts.md, measured against DISCIPLESHIP-COMPASS.md and LESSON-TEMPLATE.md.

**202-08 Marriage and Relationships** was empty and is now a full lesson. It follows the template spine: Journey Recap; the parable "Where You Go, I Will Go" (Ruth 1); teaching on covenant love received before it is given (Lamentations 3:22-23); the Contract or Covenant diagram; a "What covenant love is not" safety box; marriage as a picture of Christ and the church (Ephesians 5:31-32); "Every Season Can Be Faithful" for single, divorced and widowed readers (1 Corinthians 7:7, 7:35; Proverbs 17:17); Lesson 8 Review; and the standard 200-level closing trio. It has four mid-lesson questions. The big truth: a contract says "I will, if you will," a covenant says "I will, even when," and God keeps covenant with you first.

**202-09 Fighting for Purity** was a short stub and is now a full lesson. It follows the spine: Journey Recap; the parable "The View from the Roof" (2 Samuel 11-12, Psalm 32), in which the power and the sin stay with David; grace first ("Jesus did not just die to forgive you... He died to give you the power to fight sin"); "Purity Starts with Belonging" (1 Thessalonians 4:1, 4:3-5, plus Protect/Provide from existing research); the FIGHT plan diagram and walkthrough; "When You Have Already Fallen" (1 John 1:9, Romans 8:1, slow growth); an "If you have been harmed" box; Lesson 9 Review; and the closing trio. It has four mid-lesson questions. The TOC's existing story and tool labels are used unchanged.

**How each review point was handled:**

1. *One truth and one tool per lesson; authors only as support.* The author stacking and the six-commitments list are gone. 202-08 quotes only Keller, once. 202-09 quotes only Piper, once. The unverified popularity claim is gone.
2. *Story, images, tool.* Both stories are written. Both tools are static SVG diagrams, as the review preferred over interaction. The charcoal B&W/color story pairs could not be produced in this environment because no image generator was available, and placeholder art is disallowed. The exact Grok prompts and the ready-to-insert JSON blocks are in the two research files. **This is the open Visual gate.**
3. *Lesson Review and the 200-series closing.* Both lessons now have a Review and the key/step/prayer trio. Saved-answer handling is described below.
4. *Calendar words, prose bullets, long sentences.* No "last/next week" language remains; recaps say "in the last/next lesson" like exemplar 201-08. There are no bullet lists. Practical actions are folded into the prose and the closing step question.
5. *Marriage pastoral scope.* Reflections work for married, single, divorced and widowed readers. The safety box separates ordinary friction from abuse, threats and control, and it says covenant keeping never means staying in danger or owning another person's sin.
6. *Purity pastoral scope.* The lesson says not every private thing is sinful secrecy. It presents growth as slow rather than solved by one disclosure. It encourages voluntary help from a trusted believer, and question 9.4 says no private details are needed in stored answers.
7. *Sources.* Every Scripture quotation is exact ESV. Full verses are used in verse blocks, and short inline quotations carry references. The misattributed "flawed person" line turned out to be by Benjamin Fallon (paultripp.com, Feb 1, 2026), not Paul Tripp, and was removed. Keller was verified against several secondary sources. Piper was verified at desiringgod.org. The "Tim" in Dave's 2014 talk is unidentified, so no Tim is named. Details are in the research files. No private testimony was copied.

**TOC:** Only the 202.08 row was filled: tool "Contract or Covenant diagram", story "Ruth and Naomi (Ruth 1)", key verse Ruth 1:16, and a summary. The 202.09 row already matched and was left alone.
