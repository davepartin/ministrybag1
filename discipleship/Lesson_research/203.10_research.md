# 203.10: Your Calling: Putting It All Together

## Overview
**Key Verse:** Ephesians 4:1
**Description:** Putting it all together - finding your specific team and role in the church and in the mission.
**Goal:** Clarify your personal calling and commit to serving Christ through your church community.

## Baptist Faith & Message 2000
- Article XI: Evangelism and Missions — Every believer's calling flows from the gospel and finds expression through the church.
- Article VI: The Church — The church is the context where believers discover and exercise their calling in community.

## Recommended Research
- Baptist Faith & Message Articles VI and XI
- Os Guinness, *The Call*
- Frederick Buechner, *Wishful Thinking* on calling

## Scripture to Explore
- Ephesians 4
- 1 Corinthians 12

## Drafted 2026-09-26 (Claude, for Dave)
- **Story:** "Called by Name", Bezalel (Exodus 31:2-3; 35:34; 36:1-2). He is one of the first people Scripture describes as filled with the Spirit of God, and he is a craftsman.
- **Main ideas:**
  - Your first calling is to follow Jesus (Ephesians 4:1-3; 2:10).
  - Your particular calling shows in your SHAPE: spiritual gifts, heart, abilities, personality, experiences.
  - Putting it all together: Growing Deep, Growing Up, Growing Out.
  - When you feel unqualified (Exodus 3:11-12).
  - Calling becomes clear by serving on a team.
- **New widget:** `widgets/shape.html`, "Your SHAPE". A puzzle piece fills with S-H-A-P-E, one letter per step, then slides into its place in a row of pieces: the body of Christ (Ephesians 4:16). Buttons stay fixed, and it refits only on width changes.
- **Credit line:** the SHAPE idea from Rick Warren, *The Purpose Driven Life*.
- **Nothing church-specific:** the lesson names no particular church. It points to the 300 series, Leading in Discipleship.
