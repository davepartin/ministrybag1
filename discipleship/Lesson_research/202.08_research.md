# 202.08: Marriage and Relationships

## Overview
**Key Verse:** Ephesians 5:25. Keep it in both 202-02 and 202-08: Dave supports reusing the passage with a special focus. 202-02 applies it briefly across family roles; 202-08 unfolds covenant marriage and Christ's love for His church in 5:21-33. No change to 202-02 is needed. Final lesson approval remains with Dave.
**Big truth:** Every close relationship runs on a contract ("I will, if you will") or a covenant ("I will, even when"). God keeps covenant with you first, and Christ's self-giving love for His church is the pattern for marriage and for every covenant relationship.
**Story:** The Night God Walked Alone: God's covenant with Abram (Genesis 15).
**Tool:** Contract or Covenant diagram, `images/202.08-contract-covenant.svg`.
**Scope boundary:** 202.02 covers love across the household (spouse, parent, child, broken family stories) in one short paragraph per role. 202.08 teaches covenant love itself, unfolds Ephesians 5:21-33, and applies it to marriage, friendship and every season: single, married, divorced and widowed.

## Sources used in the lesson

- **Genesis 15 (ESV).**
  - Abram asks how he can be sure (15:2, 8).
  - The promise under the stars, quoted exactly: "Look toward heaven, and number the stars, if you are able to number them." and "So shall your offspring be" (15:5). Abram believed (15:6, paraphrased).
  - Heifer, goat and ram cut in half and laid opposite each other (15:9-10). The birds were not cut, so the lesson names only the three animals.
  - Deep sleep at sunset (15:12, paraphrased).
  - Quoted exactly: "a smoking fire pot and a flaming torch passed between these pieces" (15:17) and "On that day the LORD made a covenant with Abram" (15:18).
- **Interpretation, marked as such in the lesson.** The custom of covenant parties walking between cut animals, as a self-curse if they broke their word, is introduced as what Bible scholars explain, with "compare Jeremiah 34:18" (which describes that practice). The fire as a picture of God's presence, and God taking the full obligation on Himself, are the lesson's theological reading. Jeremiah 34:18 is referenced, not quoted, because its ESV text contains a dash.
- **Ephesians 5 (ESV).** Quoted exactly: 5:21 ("submitting to one another out of reverence for Christ"), 5:22 ("Wives, submit to your own husbands, as to the Lord"), 5:25 (a verse block; the ESV sentence continues into 5:26, so it ends with a comma) and 5:31-32 (a verse block). 5:33 is paraphrased with its reference.
- **Other ESV verses, quoted in full:** Lamentations 3:22-23, 1 Corinthians 7:7 and Proverbs 17:17. Short inline quotations with references: Luke 22:20, 1 Corinthians 7:35.
- **Dave's teaching (Obsidian sermon summaries), paraphrased in his voice:**
  - Colossians 3:18-4:1 ("Kingdom Roles at Home and Work"): submission "is not inferiority, silence, passivity, or following sin; it is honoring and helping under Jesus"; husbands love sacrificially; marriage is "a billboard of God's covenant love."
  - 1 Corinthians 7: marriage as yielding; singleness as a real calling; marriage is a blessing but not the source of ultimate satisfaction.
  - Matthew 5:27-32: contract versus covenant.
- **Tim Keller, *The Meaning of Marriage*: removed as a quotation (REV-005 item 4).** The line matched secondary sources only. A search restricted to timothykeller.com, gospelinlife.com, penguinrandomhouse.com, redeemer.com and Google Books did not establish it, and neither did the coordinator's primary search. No page number was invented. The lesson makes the "fully known and still loved" point in original prose without naming Keller. The book remains a good recommendation for couples.
- **Promise sentence (REV-005 item 3):** "At the cross, Jesus showed how far God's faithful love would go. He paid for your sin, and you can trust Him to keep His promises." Adopted from the coordinator's proposal, pending Dave's review.
- **Diagram readability (REV-005 item 1).** The Contract or Covenant SVG uses a 360-unit canvas and the lesson sets `width: 420px`. Measured smallest text is 16.8px at 375 and 17.5px at 390. The diagram was unchanged by the direction change; its "God keeps covenant with you first" band now matches the Genesis 15 opening.

## Attribution correction

The first manuscript attributed "a flawed person married to a flawed person living in a fallen world ... with a faithful God" to Paul David Tripp. The paultripp.com article carrying that line, "The (Discouraging) Definition and Location of Marriage" (Grace & Knowledge, February 1, 2026), is by **Benjamin Fallon**. It was removed. Tripp's *What Did You Expect?* (now *Marriage: 6 Gospel Commitments Every Couple Needs to Make*, Crossway) remains a good recommendation, but the lesson does not quote it.

## Pastoral safety

The "What covenant love is not" box now sits directly after the Ephesians 5 teaching. It says:

- Covenant faithfulness never means staying in danger.
- Ephesians 5 calls a husband to lay down his life, never to control, threaten or harm.
- Biblical submission never means following someone into sin or accepting abuse.
- No one is asked to cover for someone hurting them or their children, or to carry another person's sin.

It points to a pastor or safe person, "911 or your local emergency number", and the National Domestic Violence Hotline (1-800-799-7233, United States; the coordinator verified the contact).
