# 202.08: Marriage and Relationships

## Overview
**Key Verse:** Ruth 1:16
**Big truth:** Every close relationship runs on a contract ("I will, if you will") or a covenant ("I will, even when"). God keeps covenant with you first, and covenant love flows out of you after it flows in.
**Story:** Ruth and Naomi (Ruth 1).
**Tool:** Contract or Covenant diagram, `images/202.08-contract-covenant.svg`.
**Scope boundary:** 202.02 covers love across the household (spouse, parent, child, broken family stories). 202.08 teaches covenant love itself and applies it to marriage, friendship, and every season: single, married, divorced, and widowed.

## Sources used in the lesson

- **Ruth 1-4 (ESV).** Famine and move to Moab (1:1), deaths of Elimelech and both sons (1:3-5), Orpah leaves and Ruth clings (1:14), Ruth's promise (1:16, quoted in full), gleaning (ch. 2), Boaz marries Ruth (4:13), Obed, Jesse, David (4:17), and Matthew 1:5.
- **Hesed.** The Hebrew word behind "deal kindly" (Ruth 1:8), "kindness" (2:20) and Ruth's "last kindness" (3:10). The lesson cites 1:8 and 3:10.
- **Other ESV verses quoted in full:** Lamentations 3:22-23, Ephesians 5:31-32, 1 Corinthians 7:7, Proverbs 17:17. Short inline quotations are exact ESV phrases with references: Luke 22:20, 1 Corinthians 7:35.
- **Tim Keller, *The Meaning of Marriage*.** Quote: "To be loved but not known is comforting but superficial. To be known and not loved is our greatest fear. But to be fully known and truly loved is, well, a lot like being loved by God." Verified 2026-09-11 against several secondary sources (Goodreads, teachwhatisgood.com chapter summary, P.A. Early). They place it in chapter 3, "The Essence of Marriage", one citing page 101. Not checked against a printed copy; confirm the page if a citation is needed.
- **Dave's sermons (Obsidian summaries):** 1 Corinthians 7 (marriage as yielding, singleness as a real calling, "marriage is a blessing, but it is not the source of ultimate satisfaction") and Matthew 5:27-32 (contract versus covenant). The lesson paraphrases these in Dave's voice.

## Attribution correction

The first manuscript attributed "a flawed person married to a flawed person living in a fallen world ... with a faithful God" to Paul David Tripp. The paultripp.com article carrying that line, "The (Discouraging) Definition and Location of Marriage" (Grace & Knowledge, February 1, 2026), is by **Benjamin Fallon**. The quote and the "most widely read" claim were removed. Tripp's *What Did You Expect?* (now *Marriage: 6 Gospel Commitments Every Couple Needs to Make*, Crossway) remains a good recommendation for couples, but the lesson does not quote it.

## Pastoral safety

The "What covenant love is not" box makes clear that covenant faithfulness never means staying in danger, accepting abuse, threats or control, or carrying responsibility for another person's sin. It points to a pastor or safe person, 911, and the National Domestic Violence Hotline (1-800-799-7233, United States). Churches outside the US should localize that line.
