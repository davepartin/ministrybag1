# 203.06: Faith at Work and School - Your Second Space

## Overview
**Key Verse:** Daniel 6:4
**Tool:** Your Three Spaces widget (`widgets/three-spaces.html`), built on the 1st, 2nd, 3rd Spaces framework (Compass decision). 1st Space: home and close friends. 2nd Space: work and school. 3rd Space: everywhere else.
**Story:** Daniel in Babylon (Daniel 1 and 6)
**Goal:** Help the learner see work and school as a primary place to follow Jesus, living so there is "nothing to find but faith" (Daniel 6:4-5), and carry the BLESS rhythms (203.02 to 203.05) into that space.

## Scripture used (September 23, 2026 revision; Matthew 5:16 and Lamentations 3:22-23 removed at Dave's direction to keep the lesson lean)
- Daniel 1:1-9, 1:17 (exile, Babylonian school, respectful conviction, God-given skill)
- Daniel 6:3-5, 6:10, 6:22, 6:26 (faithful service, no fault but faith, steady prayer, deliverance, the king's decree)
- Genesis 2:15 (work before the fall)
- Mark 6:3 (Jesus the carpenter)
- Colossians 3:23-24 (work heartily, as for the Lord)
- Jeremiah 29:7 (seek the welfare of the city of exile)

## Pastoral notes
- Grace first: Daniel belonged to God before he served well; faithfulness at work responds to grace and does not earn it.
- The 2nd Space includes classrooms, shifts, routes and caregiving at home; the draft names people between jobs or retired.
- Workplace safety box: harassment, threats or pressure to do wrong are not something to endure silently.
- Verify every ESV quotation against esv.org before approval.
